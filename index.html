HAPT is a vocabulary of terms used to describe the conditions under which an impact assessment is, or is not, required under the GDPR and the EU AI Act. It collects the domains, purposes, processing contexts, entities, data categories, locations, technologies and consequences that appear in the Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) lists published by Member State Data Protection Authorities and in Annex III of the AI Act.

Unlike other vocabularies of this kind, HAPT records for every term the source condition it was derived from: a DPIA Required condition, a DPIA Not Required condition, or an Annex III condition. A term can come from more than one source, which makes the overlap between high-risk processing under the GDPR and high-risk AI systems under the AI Act visible at the level of individual terms.

This is a draft. The vocabulary namespace is provisional and the definitions marked † are drafts that have not yet been reviewed.

Introduction

Deciding whether a DPIA (GDPR Art. 35) or a Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (AI Act Art. 27) is required depends on describing a processing activity or AI system precisely enough to compare it with the conditions set out in law and guidance. Those conditions are expressed in terms such as evaluation of individuals, publicly accessible space, creditworthiness or emotion recognition. HAPT provides those terms as a controlled vocabulary.

HAPT is the vocabulary companion to IMPACTO. IMPACTO models the impact assessment process (necessity, procedure, outcome, notification) whilst HAPT provides the use cases involved in describing the processing and AI systems that the processes apply to. Both build on the Data Privacy Vocabulary (DPV): HAPT terms are placed under DPV parents wherever possible, and where DPV already has a term for an Annex III condition, that term is reused rather than redefined (see ).

HAPT contains 363 terms: 301 derived from the analysis of Member State DPIA lists, and 62 derived from Annex III of the AI Act. A further 86 existing DPV concepts are mapped to Annex III conditions.

Source Conditions

Each term carries one or more source-condition labels.

Source conditionMeaningTerms
DPIA Required The term was derived from a processing activity that Member State Data Protection Authorities list as requiring a DPIA under GDPR Art. 35(4), or from the EDPB guidelines on DPIA. The Source row lists the Member States whose lists contain the activity (see ).63
DPIA Not Required The term was derived from a processing activity that Member State Data Protection Authorities list as not requiring a DPIA under GDPR Art. 35(5). The Source row gives the CN identifier of each not-required processing activity the term was derived from. 211
Annex III The term was derived from Annex III of the AI Act, which lists the areas in which AI systems are high-risk under Art. 6(2). The Source row gives the Annex III point and recital, and the term is linked to the corresponding DPV eu-aiact:HighRiskAISystem-AnnexIII-* concept. Terms derived from an exception in Annex III are labelled Annex III exclusion.66
Assessment Process The term relates to the assessment process itself (AI Act Art. 27 and 46) rather than to a condition.11
Not recorded No source was recorded for the term in the previous version of the IMPACTO index, and none of its narrower terms has one. 15

Where a term had no source of its own but has narrower terms that do, the source conditions of the narrower terms are shown and the Source row says so.
